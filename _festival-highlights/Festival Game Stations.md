<p>Look out for our festival game stations at the Festival of Biodiversity
2026, where you can explore and learn more about the rich biodiversity
found in Singapore’s urban spaces.</p>
<p>Explore the festival booths and the Central Public Library. Complete all
game stations to win an exclusive FoB 2026 pin!</p>
<p></p>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Collect your activity booklet from the Info Booth or any game station.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<p></p>
<ol start="2" data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Complete the following game stations and page 7 of your booklet in any
order!</p>
</li>
</ol>

<h4>Tide N Seek</h4>
<p><strong>Play a two-part game where you uncover marine animals in sand, form food chains, and restore balance to the intertidal habitats.</strong>
</p>
</th>
<th rowspan="1" colspan="1">
<h4>Food Hoppers</h4>
<p><strong>Compete in a board game about moving around Singapore and bringing food home, while learning about “eco-connectivity”.</strong>
</p>
</th>
</tr>
<tr>
<td rowspan="1" colspan="1">
<h4>Locate The Locals</h4>
<p><strong>Assemble puzzles of native animals, and try to locate their natural habitat on the Singapore map!</strong>
</p>
</td>
<td rowspan="1" colspan="1">
<h4>Spot the Wild</h4>
<p><strong>Put your eagle eyes to the test and look out for three of our native animals in the Central Library!</strong>
</p>
